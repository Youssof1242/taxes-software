❓ Canada tax FAQ

Do higher brackets tax ALL my income?

No. Only dollars inside each band. Earning $1 more never makes you poorer — the tool's bracket tables show exactly how much falls in each band.

Which province do I pay tax in?

Your province of residence on Dec 31. If you moved mid-year, the Dec-31 province taxes your whole year's income (with special rules for business income).

What's the difference between marginal and average rate?

Marginal = tax on your next $1 (drives RRSP/overtime decisions). Average = total tax ÷ total income. The dashboard shows both.

How are dividends / capital gains taxed?

Capital gains: only 50% included in income. Dividends: grossed up (+38% eligible / +15% non-eligible) then a dividend tax credit claws most of it back — modelled in this app.

When are 2026 taxes due?

Apr 30, 2027 (Jun 15 if self-employed, but balance still due Apr 30). RRSP deadline: Mar 1, 2027. Instalments due Mar/Jun/Sep/Dec if net owing > $3,000 ($1,800 in QC).

Is this official tax filing software?

No — it's a high-accuracy estimator aligned to CRA T4127 + provincial budgets. It doesn't NETFILE. For filing, use CRA-certified software or an accountant, and always verify against canada.ca / Revenu Québec.
